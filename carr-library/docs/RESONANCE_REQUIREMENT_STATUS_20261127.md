## Pack and distribution boundary

The selected dependency identities are recorded in `packaging/windows-semantic-cp311.lock`, its CycloneDX SBOM, `windows-python.json`, `windows-ocr.lock.json`, `native-runtime/source-lock.json`, `native-launcher/toolchain.lock.json` and `windows-citations.lock.json`. They identify reviewed inputs; they do not identify an accepted final ZIP by themselves. Exact source/pack/runtime/artifact hashes must be joined by the successful final workflow and sealed manifest.

The full-audio CI environment and the no-FFmpeg library transfer profile are different artifacts.
